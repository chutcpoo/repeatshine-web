Mobile detailing repeat revenue calculator

Repeat revenue depends on how many past customers come back, what they spend, and how often they return. This calculator turns those inputs into a simple planning estimate so a mobile detailing business can see the size of its rebooking gap.

What this calculator helps you estimate

  • How many additional past customers would need to return to reach your target repeat-customer rate.
  • The revenue opportunity associated with that gap based on your average ticket and repeat visits.
  • An average monthly view to help prioritize follow-up and rebooking work.

How to use the result

Use the estimate as a prioritization signal, not a guarantee. Start with customers who are due or overdue for service, then track who was contacted, who rebooked, and who still needs follow-up. The RepeatShine Repeat Revenue System is designed for that workflow.

Is this a revenue forecast?

No. It is a planning estimate based on the values you enter. Actual rebooking behavior, pricing, seasonality, and service frequency can change the result.
